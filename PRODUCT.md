# PRODUCT.md — FoundryX

## Register
product — an iOS app (SwiftUI, Grain design system). Design serves the product.
EXCEPTION: onboarding, sign-in, and upgrade moments are brand-register islands —
they carry identity and ceremony, not chrome.

## Users
People who talk for a living and lose what they said: founders, builders,
consultants, caregivers. They wear an Omi pendant or tap Record. They are
privacy-anxious by default: the product's core promise is "your conversations
belong to you, on your devices."

## Product purpose
Ambient conversation memory that ACTS: capture → transcript → titled memory →
named speakers → answers from your own memory → an agent (default name
"Foundry") that works for you, voice-locked to its owner. The journey is a
trust ladder (see foundryx-hearth tracks/20260710T2030.../experience.md):
reward before ask; every silence is a feature; effort comes back multiplied;
nothing runs undelegated or invisibly.

## Brand personality
Calm, private, capable. A resting breath, not a product tour. Feels like: a
key being cut, a quiet colleague, a lamp-lit desk. Never feels like:
surveillance tech, a growth-hacked SaaS, a crypto app, a wellness app.

## Anti-references
- Onboarding carousels with stock illustrations and dot indicators.
- Confetti, badges, streaks, gamified anything.
- "AI sparkle" gradients (purple→pink), emoji-dense copy.
- Dashboard-y density on the phone; walls of settings.
- Dark-mode-because-tech. Theme follows iOS system setting via Grain.

## Strategic design principles
1. Ceremony at trust rungs (voice enrollment, opt-in, first nudge); utility
   everywhere else.
2. Motion = breath and light, slow exponential ease-outs; nothing bounces.
3. Privacy copy is one honest line at the moment it matters, plus one full map
   (Settings → Privacy & your data). Never a modal wall.
4. The transcript is the hero surface; color identifies speakers, not chrome.
5. On-device first: personalization and intelligence come from local models;
   the design should FEEL local (instant, silent, no spinners for network).
